# Embroidery comes to life

A 30-second film that plays live in the browser. A wooden hoop holds linen; an unseen needle stitches a fox, a bird, a child with a kite, grass and a sun, and then the stitched figures move in stitches, 12 steps a second.

- Open `index.html`. The film starts muted and loops.
- Space pauses. Hover to show the scrub bar. "Play with sound" restarts the film with its score.
- Click the linen to stitch a flower. Move the mouse to slide the light across the thread.
- `?t=<seconds>` freezes a moment; `?view=wide|top|fox|kite|child|low` picks a camera.

Raw WebGL2 and WebAudio, no libraries and no image assets. Every stitch is geometry made in code.
